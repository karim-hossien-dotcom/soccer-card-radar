# Veriswap (workstream 3 of 3)

Karim's logged-in Veriswap (Chrome). A few page views per run, no crawling. Asks are owner-set, not values:
every offer is built on our comps (card-comps rules).

## Goal
Trade the smaller graded cards up into liquid big-name slabs, then toward the Ronaldo Obsidian auto (BGS 8.5).
Current targets, bundles and offers live in card-mcp research/2026-11-17-veriswap-trade-up-plan.md (moves to
veriswap/ with the next rewrite); targets come from the Vault's snipe list and researcher picks.

## Valuing both sides
- Each of our cards at the comp for ITS OWN grade (Vault). Never another grade's newest comp.
- Their card at our comp for that exact card and grade (Vault or SnipeTarget), not their ask.
- Card.protected = true: never in a bundle or offer unless Karim approves that deal for a bigger card.

## Offer rules
- Their side at most 10% over our comps (beyond that, the eBay route wins after fees).
- Each side at least about $70 (fee 2.5% graded, 3.2% raw, plus a $5-20 inbound label). Vault-to-vault 1.8%.
- Graded only on the incoming side; vault the incoming slab.
- Prefer counterparties with completed trades and no failed follow-throughs; flag owners with no history.
- Offer 10-15% more value when the other side trades down, using cards whose cost is low against their value.
- Ship within 2 business days of acceptance (penalty $200 or 80%).
- Nothing is sent, accepted, countered or repriced without Karim's yes for that offer ("send 1, 3").

## Daily run (report only)
Incoming offers with a verdict; replies to our interests; cheapest asks on each target vs our comp; Trading Floor
"Wants to Trade Down" leads; a numbered ready-to-send list with every card's own-grade comp shown.
